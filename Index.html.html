A few useful details

Only if you really want a meet & greet

Wristbands start at 8:30 AM

Some Meet & Greets are marked “Wristbanded.” Free wristbands are distributed in Concourse C Lobby each event morning, first-come, first-served, while supplies last.

Everyone needs their own wristband. Separate lines are used for different sessions. A wristband guarantees a spot only if you arrive at least 30 minutes before that session ends. No standby or overflow line.

Official wristband rules ↗ Meet & Greet schedule ↗

Badge & entry

Bring and wear your physical badge on a lanyard. A digital QR code alone won't work for entry. If your badge wasn't mailed, use Registration/Will Call.

Your Legendary VIP dedicated morning queue is useful for morning entry, but is not confirmed as priority access to wristband distribution.

Find your way

Use the official MagicCon site and mobile app for last-minute schedule changes, maps, and exact locations. Some experiences have limited capacity.
